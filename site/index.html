Ready in a minute

A home in your toolbar.

  1. Download and unzip the extension.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome 116 or later.
  3. Turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, then select the extracted tracelet folder.
  4. Pin Tracelet. Open a website, then click Record this tab.

Developer installation. Tracelet is not listed in the Chrome Web Store.

Your recording stays yours

No account.
No cloud recording.

Video and tasks stay in this Chrome profile until you export or delete them. Nothing is sent to a server. No microphone, analytics or AI provider.

Visible private information is recorded too. Close sensitive content before recording. Removing a task step does not redact the video. Typed field values are not collected; you enter them when replaying.

Tracelet processes captured actions into tasks. It does not claim AI video understanding. Replay stays on the original page, one approved step at a time; check each website result. Navigation ends capture. Recordings are limited to five minutes, 100 MiB and 500 actions.
